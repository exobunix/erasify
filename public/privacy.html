Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our utmost priority. Read how Erasify by Avdar Innovations Pvt Limited protects your data with local client-side processing.

Effective Date: October 9, 2026 • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

Zero Media Retention Guarantee

Your photos and videos never leave your device. Erasify executes computer vision algorithms directly in your web browser via WebAssembly and Web Workers. We do not store, view, or transmit your media to any cloud storage or remote servers.

1. Overview & Service Provider

This Privacy Policy explains the collection, usage, and safeguarding of information when you access the Erasify platform (accessible via web and mobile applications).

Erasify is owned and operated by Avdar Innovations Pvt Limited (“we”, “us”, or “our”), a premier bespoke software, web, and artificial intelligence development firm.

2. Client-Side Sandbox & Media Privacy

Unlike conventional media utilities that transmit your files to cloud computing farms:

  • Client-Side Engine: Watermark detection and inpainting occur strictly within your browser’s local runtime sandbox using WebAssembly (Wasm), SIMD, and client Canvas pipelines.
  • No Server Uploads: When you select an image or video file, it is loaded into your local computer or phone RAM. It is never uploaded to Erasify’s backend.
  • No Content Inspection: We cannot see, analyze, catalog, sell, or retain the photos or videos you process.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal data required to manage user accounts and quotas:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, and encrypted password credentials.
  • Usage & Subscription Metrics: We track your active plan type (Free, Daily, Unlimited, Basic) and the numerical counter of operations performed to respect service quota limits.
  • Technical Metadata: Standard non-identifying server logs (browser type, timestamp, referral headers) collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure to maintain service availability and prevent abuse.

4. How We Use Information

We use personal data strictly for:

  • Authenticating your session and remembering your logged-in state.
  • Tracking and refreshing daily or lifetime tier quotas.
  • Responding to your direct inquiries or support tickets submitted via our Contact portal or WhatsApp channel.
  • Preventing malicious misuse, DDOS attacks, or automated credential stuffing.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Erasify utilizes secure, HTTP-only session cookies (such as session_email) solely to keep you signed in securely. We also utilize browser localStorage to store client-side app preferences, such as cached model flags. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell ad-tracking data to data brokers.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

To deliver an ultra-fast and reliable platform, we rely on trusted infrastructure providers who follow stringent security standards:

  • Vercel: Global CDN edge hosting and serverless API execution.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted enterprise cloud database hosting for user authentication credentials and quota states.

None of these third parties are ever granted access to your processed images or video assets.

7. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Regardless of your jurisdiction (including GDPR, CCPA, and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection standards), you have the right to:

  • Access the profile and email information associated with your account.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and related usage data.
  • Opt out of any marketing or operational communications.

To request account or data deletion, contact us directly via our phone, WhatsApp, or through the Contact Us form. Requests are processed within 48 hours.
